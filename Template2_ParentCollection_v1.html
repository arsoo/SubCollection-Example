How to Choose the Right Binding Machine

Not sure which binding style is right for you? Here's a quick guide to the 8 most popular binding methods available at MyBinding.com.

Method 01
Plastic Comb
Quick and simple, comb binding offers a basic closure for documents up to 425 pages that can lay flat for reading or photocopying.
Method 02
Color Coil / Spiral
A popular method used for marketing materials, reports, and more. Coils come in over 50 colors and allow full 360° page rotation.
Method 03
Twin-Loop Wire
A great method if you need your documents to look professional and presentable. Wires come in a handful of colors including silver and bronze.
Method 04
Thermal
Perfect for documents requiring a highly polished look. Simply place the pages in a hardback or paperback cover and the machine does the rest.
Method 05
Fastback
Offers a sleek and slim finish ideal for mailing, storage, and stacking. Documents have a professional perfect-bound look.
Method 06
Combination
These machines are a great tool if you need to put together both internal documents and client presentations using multiple binding styles.
Method 07
VeloBind
If you're looking for security, this binding keeps documents secure and un-editable with a strong, durable spine for permanent binding.
Method 08
ProClick
GBC ProClick machines and spines give documents a finished, professional look while still allowing easy editing — the best of both worlds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about binding machines.

What is the difference between manual and electric binding machines?
+
Manual binding machines require physical effort to punch holes and open binding elements — ideal for low-to-medium volume use. Electric binding machines automate the punching process with a motor, significantly reducing user fatigue and increasing speed, making them ideal for high-volume offices and print shops.
What types of documents can I bind with these machines?
+
Our binding machines handle a wide range of documents including presentations, reports, training manuals, catalogs, textbooks, yearbooks, legal briefs, and more. Different binding styles suit different needs — comb and wire binding work great for editable documents, while thermal and perfect binding create a polished book-like finish.
How do I choose the right binding machine for my business?
+
Consider your daily volume, the type of finish you need (editable vs. permanent), your document size (letter vs. legal), and your budget. For occasional use, a manual comb or coil machine works well. For frequent or high-volume use, an electric machine saves time and effort. Call us at 800-944-4573 for expert guidance.
Are binding machines compatible with different types of combs and covers?
+
Yes. Most binding machines are compatible with a wide range of supplies. Comb machines use standard 19-hole plastic combs. Wire machines use twin-loop wire in various sizes. Coil machines work with spiral coils in many colors and diameters. We carry all compatible supplies to match your machine.
